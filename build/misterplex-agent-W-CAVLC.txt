Table-validation and round-trip approach reported before deep RTL:
1. Keep CAVLC table decode in shipped RTL modules, added to fpga/Plex_MiSTer/files.qip; testbench-only wrappers live under tests/rtl and are not synthesized.
2. Validate the spec tables mechanically in the C++/Verilator harness before driving RTL: for coeff_token (nC tables 0..3 plus chroma-DC), total_zeros (4x4 plus chroma-DC), and run_before, every nonzero-length code is checked for uniqueness and prefix-freeness.
3. The harness includes a reference CAVLC encoder. It builds synthetic coefficient vectors that deliberately cover every coeff_token entry, every luma/chroma total_zeros entry, every run_before entry including zerosLeft>=7 escape-style rows, trailing-one signs, and suffixLength escalation with large signed levels.
4. Each encoded residual block is decoded by the real RTL artifact h264_cavlc_residual_block.sv via Verilator. A testbench-only top can perturb output behavior for mandatory red proof; no perturbation ifdefs are placed in shipped RTL.
5. Coefficients are emitted in CAVLC scan order, matching h264_dequant4x4 coeff[0:15]. Decoder is sequential (bit/table/register states) with registered output, so it does not add a wide combinational VLC cone into the already timing-sensitive dequant→IDCT→recon path.
