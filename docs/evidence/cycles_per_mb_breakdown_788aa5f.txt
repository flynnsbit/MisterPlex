  A3. Serial I16 pred = 256 cy (1 px/cy) + 1 start gap.
  A4. Serial I16 DC Hadamard ≈ 16..20 cy (order of 16; exact latency UNCERTAIN ±4).

  A6. Frame-0 mix: many I_4x4 and I_16x16 MBs; residual density high

---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. VERDICT (plain)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  MEASURED: ~4037 cy/MB frame0 @ 320×240  vs  budget ~2000  →  OVER ~2.0×

  Trading area for throughput is REAL and visible now.
  Further serialisation (traverse tc_top/i4_mode_top nC) adds little cy/MB
  if it stays combinational→RAM on the parse side (CAVLC already sequential);
  neighbour-side serialisation already dominates sink.

  Headroom for MORE sink serialisation: NEGATIVE vs 2k budget.
  Headroom for traverse fabric→RAM (no extra sequential loops on paint): OK
  if it does not add per-pixel RMW.

  If product needs 23 fps @ 50 MHz on 320×240 I-frames, either:
    (a) raise clock / accept lower FPS on IDR, or
    (b) re-parallelise selected paint paths after ALM fit is won, or
    (c) overlap traverse parse with sink paint more aggressively
        (today res_blk_ready = ST_IDLE only — no block pipelining).

  Option (c) is the highest-leverage throughput recovery that does NOT add DSP
  and may even hide some of the 4k by dual-buffering one MB plane.
