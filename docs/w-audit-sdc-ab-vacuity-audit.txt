Findings
--------

   identical.

   The generated build_id.v is identical, so BUILD_DATE/generation metadata is
   not an alternate explanation for the different RBFs.

2. The earlier "SDC neutrality" control was indeed vacuous with respect to the
   relevant independent variable.  The slots that all fit to fb4bad84 all share
   the first four CDC constraints as finite set_max_delay constraints, not the
   old set_false_path constraints:

     wfit-hour27-sdc-a/b: false_paths=0 max_delay=5 diag_max=0 -> fb4bad84
     wfit-hour27-bdiag-a/b: false_paths=0 max_delay=6 diag_max=1 -> fb4bad84

   Therefore those controls prove determinism across repeated/new-SDC builds,
   and also show the extra ddr_frame_store diagnostic max_delay is not what
   changes the RBF.  They do not test the old false_path SDC against the new
   finite-bound SDC.  The real changed variable between 3b1e8435 and fb4bad84 is
   the conversion of the four clk_sys/clk_ddr CDC cuts from set_false_path to
   set_max_delay.

3. The correction should be worded precisely.  Different SDC-only inputs produce
   different programming files and different fitted resource accounting:

     FIT_KEY_DIFF only_a=0 only_b=0 common=827
     FIT_RESOURCE_DIFFS 39
     top util A: ALMs 17702, block bits 2970061, M10K 453, DSP 74
     top util B: ALMs 17706, block bits 2970061, M10K 453, DSP 74

   That refutes "implementation-neutral" and "RBF-neutral".  It does not, by
   itself, prove a functional RTL-equivalent netlist changed; a constraint-only
   change can also alter packing/routing/retiming/resource accounting.  The safe
   claim is: the SDC change is not neutral to the fitted implementation/RBF, and
   a hardware A/B of 3b1e8435 vs fb4bad84 must be read as old-SDC vs new-SDC, not
   as an RTL-window test.

4. Secondary trap found: there are three SDC variants among the historical slots,
   not just two.

     old:       9a312bcb, 5 false_paths, 1 max_delay, diag false_path
     new-lite:  6b255728, 0 false_paths, 5 max_delay, no diag max_delay
     new-bdiag: 13e7312e, 0 false_paths, 6 max_delay, diag max_delay

   new-lite and new-bdiag both fit to fb4bad84.  This isolates the harmlessness
   of the diagnostic max_delay addition for these builds, but does not rescue the
   old neutrality claim because neither new-lite nor new-bdiag contains the old
   four CDC false paths.

Conclusion
----------
BROKE/NARROWED: the published shorthand "SDC change changed the netlist" is too
strong if "netlist" means functional logic.  The evidence proves SDC-only input
changes alter the fitted implementation/RBF and small resource counts.

COULD NOT BREAK: W-FIT's corrected strategic ruling that 3b1e8435 is a constraints
vs RTL A/B, not a clk_ddr RTL-window A/B.  The prior exoneration was a control
that did not vary the relevant SDC variable.
